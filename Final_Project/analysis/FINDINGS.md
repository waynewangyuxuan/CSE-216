# Bet 8 findings, before the skeptic

Each finding names the file that holds its numbers. All numbers come from `scripts/run_all.sh`. Categories are literature-grounded inferences from AI-coded behavior (codebook v1.2 §9), not measures of cognition, and no human coded or checked anything. "Support" means `OFFLOADING` + `AUGMENTING`. "Whole sessions" are NIRVANA 71 + WildChat (Bet 7) 35, 498 windows; Bet 4's 30 WildChat windows are incomplete sessions and are left out of session measures.

The skeptic's verdict on each finding is in `SKEPTIC.md`; the report uses only what survived, with the counterpoint stated.

| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Frozen v1.2 keeps its coverage out of sample: 336 of 428 new windows (78.5%) get a category other than `INDETERMINATE`, against 79/100 in sample. NIRVANA new windows 85.6%, WildChat 65.6%. C0 windows are 13.6% covered, C2 93.6% | `results/out_of_sample.md` §1, §4 |
| F2 | About a third of whole-session windows are surrender-leaning (167/498, 33.5%) and a third support (175/498, 35.1%). Most surrender-leaning is weak: 112 of 182 across all 528 windows. In WildChat 67 of 69 surrender-leaning windows rest on R16 (request type alone), so there it mostly means "asked the AI to write it". With R16 treated as indeterminate, the surrender-leaning share falls to 14.3% | `results/out_of_sample.md` §2–3; `results/sensitivity.md` |
| F3 | Strong surrender-leaning (R09: AI text pasted with no visible own work and no check, editor visible) is rare: 15 windows, all NIRVANA | `results/out_of_sample.md` §2–3 |
| F4 | Sessions sort into types: 34 surrender-leaning only, 23 mixed, 40 support only, 9 neither (of 106) | `results/patterns.md` §2 |
| F5 | Surrender-leaning → surrender-leaning is the commonest transition (83 of 143 out of SL, 58%), but most of that is session make-up, not order. Shuffling categories within each session gives 76.9 (p 0.045); in NIRVANA alone, observed equals shuffled (40 vs 40.1, p 0.59). WildChat runs exceed the shuffle (43 vs 36.7, p 0.001) | `results/sessions.md`; `figures/fig05` |
| F6 | Switching between surrender-leaning and support is symmetric: 29 adjacent moves each way once `INDETERMINATE` and `NEUTRAL_MECHANICAL` windows are removed, in 23 sessions. Among sessions with both, support comes first in 10 and surrender-leaning in 13. No drift toward surrender within sessions is visible | `results/sessions.md` |
| F7 | When a session has a surrender-leaning window that is not its last, 19 of 50 later reach a support window (38%). NIRVANA 17 of 29 (59%); WildChat 2 of 21 (10%). WildChat cannot show the editor behaviors that make a window support-side (U3, U7), so the gap is at least partly coverage | `results/sessions.md`; `figures/fig08` |
| F8 | Surrender-leaning, when present, usually starts at the first window: 33 of 52 multi-window sessions with any SL have it at window 1 (NIRVANA 16/31, WildChat 17/21). Its share falls across the session (first fifth to last fifth, `figures/fig06`) | `results/sessions.json` `first_sl_relative`, `position_bins` |
| F9 | Escalation from writer-given content (`D1`) to taking an AI proposal (`D4`) occurs in 6 of 71 NIRVANA sessions (e25, e26, e31, e44, e70, e74); WildChat has no `D4` window at all | `results/patterns.md` §4; `results/inputs/labels_final.csv` |
| F10 | Code signatures map to categories consistently, except "asked and moved on" (`U8`): whole paste → SL 30/32; partial take (`D3`) → OFF 37/46; ask then write own text (`D2 U7`) → AUG or OFF 44/52; draft kept in chat → SL 69/72; `U8` splits across all five categories (SL 48 of 102), because the rules read it by request type | `results/patterns.md` §1 |
| F11 | Reasoned checks (`V3`/`V4`) are rare: 13 windows in 6 sessions, all NIRVANA. `V2` (AI checks itself) 5 windows | `results/patterns.md` §1 |
| F12 | Categories do not hinge on the adjudicator: rules applied to coder A's labels alone and to coder B's alone agree on 471/528 windows (κ 0.86). Final categories match A on 502 and B on 470, but the surrender-leaning count is 193 under A, 181 under B, 182 final | `results/out_of_sample.md` §7 |
| F13 | 20 windows have an `UNSETTLED` field and go to R00. 6 of them could be surrender-leaning under some reading; dropping all 20 moves the surrender-leaning and support shares by under 2 points (coverage rises 3.3 points, since R00 windows are indeterminate by definition) | `results/out_of_sample.md` §5; `results/sensitivity.md` |
