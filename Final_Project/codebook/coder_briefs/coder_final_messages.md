# What the pilot coders said was hardest

Paraphrased after the fact from each coder's final message. The originals were not saved, so this is a summary, not a record. Rounds 1–5. No window text.

| Round | Coder | Hardest rules, in their order |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | A | `D4` vs `D2`/`D3`/`DX` when typed text echoes the AI, with no threshold for overlap; which decision is focal under a thin stance. `U4` vs `UX` when a next turn says nothing about the output. What counts as `V3`, and where a challenge belongs |
| R1 | B | Decision codes when typed text follows an AI suggestion. Uptake when the next turn is a new request. Operation and function on mixed requests |
| R2 | A | `DX` vs `D1`/`D2` when input is missing. `D1` and `prior_own_work` when supplied text came from the AI. `U4` vs `U8`, and `V1` vs `V3`, on partial reactions |
| R2 | B | `U4` vs `U8` when a later turn reuses the output. `D1` vs `D0` vs `D2` on re-polished AI text. `V1` vs `V3` on style complaints; `U1` vs `U2` on short edits |
| R3 | A | `V3` on thin grounds, including a pasted run error. `D1` vs AI-origin for summaries. `operation` for fact lookups and code tasks |
| R3 | B | `D1` vs `D2` when the AI builds on supplied material. `U1` vs `U2` vs `U3` on mixed pastes. `V3` vs `V0` vs `V4` on thin challenges |
| R4 | A | `D1` vs AI-origin when the AI evaluates supplied material or reworks an earlier rendering. `U4` vs `U8` on turns near the output; praise plus "continue". `operation` and `function` on bare or poorly fitting requests |
| R4 | B | `U4` vs `U5` vs `U8` when the next request builds on this output. Editor pastes that match an earlier response. Bare requests and `F2`/`F4`, `O02`/`O09`/`O12` lines |
| R5 | A | Summary vs rewording when a writer condenses their own text. A next chat turn resubmitting a blend of AI wording and silent edits. `VISIBLE_DRAFT` vs `VISIBLE_PARTIAL` on one sentence |
| R5 | B | `U8` vs `U4` when a next turn resends a revised paragraph. `D1` vs `D2` for content built on notes. `F1` vs `F5` when the owner of the text is unclear |
