**What this means.**

- `V2`, `V3` and `V4` have few real cases, so "two coders can apply them" rests mostly on 12 cases I constructed.
- Field-level `verification` κ is high because `V0` dominates. On the check labels themselves, coders agree less.

- Sample and gold could differ. I did not look.
